# NEURO-METRO: AVUI
## System Design Document: Combat Mechanics
**Document Scope:** Combat Module (Sibling to Meta + Map Exploration)
**Platform:** Mobile (Portrait Orientation)
**Core Paradigm:** Turn-Based, 1 Player vs. 1-3 Enemies

---

### 1. HIGH-LEVEL OVERVIEW
The combat system in *Neuro-Metro: AVUI* centers around emotional volatility. It is a dual-resource tightrope where taking damage fuels the player's capacity to act, but accumulating too much fuel forces a catastrophic loss of control. The system demands constant mitigation, anticipation, and manipulation of both the Player's and Enemies' emotional thresholds.

### 2. CORE RESOURCE ECONOMY
Both Player and Enemies operate on the exact same resource rules.

#### Mental Stamina (MS)
*   **Definition:** The unit's Health Points (HP) and their maximum Emotional Capacity.
*   **Function:** Dictates how much punishment a unit can take, and how much Emotional Charge they can safely hold.
*   **Defeat Condition:** If MS reaches 0, the unit is defeated (Player loses, or Enemy dies).
*   **Damage Intake:** Receiving an attack permanently reduces MS for the duration of the encounter.

#### Emotional Charge (EC)
*   **Definition:** The action resource (Mana/Stamina) required to cast abilities.
*   **Function:** Volatile fuel used for attacks, defenses, heals, buffs, and debuffs.
*   **Generation:** Primarily generated by *receiving* damage or via specific charging abilities.
*   **Consumption:** Spent by casting abilities.

**The Golden Rule:** `0 < EC < MS`
A unit must maintain their EC between 0 and their current MS to remain in control and combat-effective.

---

### 3. EXTREME STATES (STATUS CONDITIONS)

#### Emotional Exhaustion (EC = 0)
When a unit spends their last point of EC, they enter Exhaustion.
*   **Offensive Penalty:** The unit cannot cast any abilities that cost EC.
*   **Defensive Penalty:** The unit receives a severe vulnerability modifier (e.g., +50% incoming MS damage).
*   **Resolution:** The unit must use a "Rest" action (Skip turn, gain flat EC) or wait to be hit by an enemy attack that generates EC.

#### Emotional Overload (EC ≥ MS)
When a unit's EC exceeds or equals their current MS, they Overload.
*   **Loss of Control:** The unit loses agency for 1 to 3 turns.
*   **Unpredictable Behavior:** The AI takes over, casting random abilities from the unit's pool.
*   **Consequences:** The unit might cast high-cost attacks on sub-optimal targets, cast heals on the enemy, or burn their EC down to 0, instantly shifting from Overload to Exhaustion.
*   **Resolution:** Control is returned once the Overload turn duration expires or if EC is reduced below MS via enemy siphon abilities.

---

### 4. EMOTIONAL TYPES (CLASSES/ROLES)
Units belong to an Emotional Type, dictating their base stats, ability pool constraints, and passive traits.

*   **Fury (Aggro/Burst):** 
    *   *Passive:* Gains bonus EC when MS drops below 30%. 
    *   *Weakness:* Takes more MS damage from Apathy.
*   **Apathy (Control/Tank):** 
    *   *Passive:* Natural damage mitigation, but generates less EC from incoming attacks. Requires self-harm abilities to fuel EC.
    *   *Weakness:* Susceptible to Euphoria buffs.
*   **Euphoria (Support/Engine):** 
    *   *Passive:* High base MS. Abilities often manipulate both Player and Enemy EC simultaneously.
    *   *Weakness:* Low direct MS damage output.
*   **Sorrow (Debuff/Sustain):** 
    *   *Passive:* Lifesteal mechanics (converting enemy MS loss into player MS regen).
    *   *Weakness:* Extreme vulnerability during Exhaustion.

---

### 5. ABILITY POOL & DECKBUILDING
The player curates a limited loadout of abilities prior to encounters.
*   **Fixed Costs:** Abilities cost a fixed amount of EC.
*   **Dual Targeting:** Abilities can target MS (raw damage), EC (resource manipulation), or both.
*   **Examples:**
    *   *Venting Strike (Cost: 30 EC):* Deals 25 MS damage. Generates 10 EC for the target.
    *   *Gaslight (Cost: 15 EC):* Deals 0 MS damage. Forces the target to gain 40 EC (pushes towards Overload).
    *   *Catharsis (Cost: 60 EC):* Heals 30 MS. Clears all debuffs. 
    *   *Repress (Cost: 0 EC):* Rest action. Reduces own EC by 20. Gain 'Shield' for 1 turn.

### 6. COMPLEX GAMEPLAY EXAMPLES

#### Example A: The Overload Push
**Setup:** 
*   **Player (Type: Fury):** 100 MS | 50 EC
*   **Enemy (Type: Apathy):** 60 MS | 45 EC

**Turn 1: Player**
*   *Observation:* The enemy is dangerously close to their limit (45 EC / 60 MS).
*   *Action:* Player casts *Gaslight* (Cost: 15 EC). Targets Enemy EC.
*   *Result:* 
    *   Player: 100 MS | 35 EC
    *   Enemy: 60 MS | 85 EC. 
*   *System Check:* Enemy EC (85) > Enemy MS (60). **Enemy enters Overload for 2 turns.**

**Turn 1: Enemy (Overloaded)**
*   *Action:* AI randomly selects *Self-Destructive Lash*.
*   *Result:* Enemy spends 60 EC, deals 10 MS damage to Player, but hits itself for 10 MS.
*   *State Update:* 
    *   Enemy: 50 MS | 25 EC. 
    *   Player: 90 MS | 45 EC (+10 EC from taking hit).

#### Example B: The Exhaustion Trap
**Setup:**
*   **Player (Type: Sorrow):** 80 MS | 40 EC
*   **Enemy A:** 100 MS | 20 EC
*   **Enemy B:** 100 MS | 20 EC

**Turn 1: Player**
*   *Action:* Player wants to clear Enemy A quickly. Casts *Grief Cleave* (Cost: 40 EC). Deals 50 MS damage.
*   *State Update:* Player is now at 80 MS | 0 EC. **Player enters Exhaustion.**

**Turn 1: Enemy A**
*   *Action:* Casts *Numb* (Cost: 10 EC). Deals 10 MS damage. 
*   *Calculation:* Player is Exhausted (+50% damage taken modifier). 10 * 1.5 = 15 MS damage.
*   *State Update:* Player: 65 MS | 15 EC. (Player took 15 MS damage, gained 15 EC). **Player is no longer Exhausted.**

**Turn 1: Enemy B**
*   *Action:* Casts *Mockery* (Cost: 20 EC). Deals 25 MS damage.
*   *State Update:* Player: 40 MS | 40 EC (Player took 25 MS damage, gained 25 EC). 
*   *Outcome:* The Player survived but lost half their MS capacity because they miscalculated their EC burn and left themselves vulnerable to Enemy A's multiplier.
