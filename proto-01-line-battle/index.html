HOW TO PLAY

Emotional Entities wander the line.
Confront them without losing yourself.

1
CONFRONT Break the Entity’s emotional layers before it breaks yours.
2
SUMMON Spend Emotional Charge (EC) to summon abilities onto your Metro Line. It runs right to left.
3
MATCH Match a layer’s emotion and it absorbs the blow — half damage, and it charges them. Strip them all and hits land double.
4
REGULATE EC is your fuel. Mental Stamina (MS) is your life and the ceiling your EC must stay under. Damage lowers MS, closing the ceiling in.
5
OVERLOAD Let EC pass MS and you lose control — your Line fills with Self Harm and Feed, and you cannot remove them.
